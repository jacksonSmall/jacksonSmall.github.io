01 · About

Statistical theory, shipped to production

I'm a data scientist working at the intersection of statistical theory and production ML — building things that actually ship. I graduated from UCF's Burnett Honors College with a B.S. in Data Science (GPA 3.83, May 2026) and start the M.S. in Statistics & Data Science in Fall 2026 as a Graduate Teaching Assistant.

At UCF's AIM Lab (AI & Imaging in Medicine), under director Dr. Yu Tian, I research zero-shot autoregressive video generation for real-time anomaly and crime prediction — feeding surveillance frames into Wan 2.2 and scoring how reality diverges from the predicted future. I enter grad school on a Graduate Teaching Assistantship ($15K stipend) plus the competitive Graduate Dean's Fellowship ($5K) with full tuition remission.
